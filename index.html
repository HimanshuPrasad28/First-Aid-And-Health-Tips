First Aid Procedures

CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation)

+

Follow these steps for adult CPR:

  1. Check the scene for safety
  2. Check responsiveness
  3. Call 911
  4. Open the airway
  5. Check for breathing
  6. Begin chest compressions
  7. Provide rescue breaths
  8. Continue CPR until help arrives

Choking

+

For a choking adult or child:

  1. Stand behind the person
  2. Wrap your arms around their waist
  3. Make a fist with one hand
  4. Position it above the navel
  5. Grasp your fist with the other hand
  6. Press into the abdomen with quick upward thrusts
  7. Repeat until the object is expelled

Bleeding Control

+

To control severe bleeding:

  1. Apply direct pressure with a clean cloth
  2. Maintain pressure for 15 minutes
  3. Add more cloth if needed, don't remove soaked material
  4. If possible, elevate the wound above the heart
  5. Apply a tight bandage when bleeding slows
  6. Seek medical attention immediately
